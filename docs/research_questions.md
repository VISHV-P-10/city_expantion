## What is urban sprawl?
Urban sprawl is the rapid, unplanned, and uncoordinated expansion of cities and towns into surrounding rural areas, characterized by low-density residential housing, single-use zoning, and a heavy reliance on private automobiles.

## What is Land use and Land cover
Land cover refers to the actual physical material present on the surface, such as forests, water bodies, bare soil, or urban infrastructure.  Land use describes how humans utilize that land, such as for agriculture, residential development, or recreation

## what is remote sensing
Remote sensing is the science and art of acquiring information about an object, area, or phenomenon without making physical contact with it.  This technology detects and measures reflected or emitted radiation (such as visible light, infrared, or microwaves) from a distance, typically using sensors mounted on satellites, aircraft, or drones

## what is raster data

Raster data is a spatial data model that represents geographic information as a grid of cells (pixels) organized in rows and columns, where each cell contains a value representing a specific attribute, such as color, elevation, or temperature.  Unlike vector data, which uses points, lines, and polygons to define discrete features, raster data is ideal for representing continuous phenomena that change gradually across a landscape, such as satellite imagery, digital elevation models, and temperature gradients.

## what is spatial resolution 
Spatial resolution is the measure of the smallest object or detail that can be distinguished in an image, typically defined by the ground area represented by a single pixel.  In remote sensing and GIS, it refers to the physical dimensions on the Earth's surface (e.g., 10 meters per pixel), where smaller pixel sizes indicate higher resolution and greater detail

## what is temporal resolution 
In remote sensing, it refers to the revisit rate, or how frequently a satellite captures imagery of a specific area, ranging from multiple times per day for high-resolution systems to every 16 days for others like Landsat
